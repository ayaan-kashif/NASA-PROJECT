## P5 — Bolts and torque (2026-11-12)
- Core `BoltMath` / `BoltJointState` (pure, used by VAB, HOUSTON and flight): StarOrder(N) (1-5-3-7-2-6-4-8, 1-7-4-10-2-8-5-11-3-9-6-12), states Missing/Inserted/Torqued/Under/Over, passes 30/70/100%, warp detection (bolt fully torqued while a neighbour is below 30%), integrity = torqued share − warp penalty (max 0.35) − 0.15 per stripped bolt. `JointStatus.FailureQkPa = maxQkPa × integrity` for Prompt 10. Saved in `VehicleDesign.bolts` + `jointWarps`; `BoltJointState.AllStatuses(rules, vehicle)` gives every joint's status.
- VAB: bolts sit on an external flange (BOLT_## children). Parts Bin lists JOINTS; click one -> camera goes to the flange, Bolt panel shows spec, pass, star order + next bolt, counts, integrity bar. Click hole = fit bolt; hold LMB = torque with a circular gauge (green target band, ratchet click every 10%); click wrench stops at the pass preset, Manual can strip a bolt; RMB removes. Next bolt pulses green. A part can't be detached while its joint still has bolts.
- Verified: star 3 passes = integrity 1.00; clockwise full torque = warp toasts + 0.65; 28/28 EditMode tests.
