T-MINUS

Build it bolt by bolt. Wire it pin by pin. Launch it for real.

Play in your browser

Desktop Chrome, Edge or Firefox with a keyboard and mouse. About 26 MB to load. Touch screens are experimental: one finger clicks and drags, two fingers orbit the camera, pinching zooms.

What you do

  • Build a 3U CubeSat and wire every power, data and radio cable. Watch the power, data and mass budgets change live.
  • Stack and bolt a two-stage rocket in the right order, then wire its avionics and pyro channels.
  • Run the pre-launch checklist with up to four crew roles, then launch with real physics.
  • Operate the satellite for 30 days and get a debrief that names the cause of every failure and shows real Landsat images of the 2022 Pakistan floods.

For teachers

Schools: what is free, and how to join the pilot

  • Free, no account, no ads. Play progress stays on the student's own computer.
  • Every number has a one-line reason and a Space Guide topic with a source and a quick check.
  • Failure Lab repair jobs (including a real Apollo 12 console call) make good 10-minute starters.
  • The debrief's Export results (CSV) button copies a student's attempts. Paste them all into the class results page to see scores, who reached orbit and which causes lost the most missions. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Text size, low-graphics and reduce-motion options are in Settings. HOUSTON, the in-game helper, can be set to hints only or off.
